Calm Space — sensory calm, fidgets & talk cards

A one-page guide for care services

Calm Space is free and not-for-profit. No ads, no accounts, no tracking and nothing to buy. Everything is kept only on the device, and it works offline. It was made by a former SEN teacher for autistic and non-verbal people, their families, schools and care services.

1. Set it up (5 minutes, once per device)

  1. Add it to the home screen so it opens full-screen and works with no internet. iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. Open it once while online so the voice and sounds are saved for offline use.
  2. Open Settings (the gear at the top of Home). An adult check keeps children out of it. Choose who it's for, colour strength, sound, text size and the voice (Isabella, Emma, George or Daniel; each has a Hear button).
  3. Turn on Guided Access (iPhone/iPad: Settings › Accessibility) or Screen pinning (Android: Settings › Security) if the person should stay inside Calm Space.

2. What's in it

  • Calm scenes and fidgets: slow light and sound, plus pop, spin, flick and squish. Nothing flashes: never more than two big brightness changes a second, no red flashes, brightness and volume are capped.
  • Talk cards and Yes / No: tap a picture to say it aloud. Adults can add photo cards, rename cards or hide ones that aren't needed.
  • My day: a Now / Next / Later or First / Then picture timetable with a gentle ring timer (no alarm, no ticking).
  • Feelings and stories: a feelings scale, 16 faces and short social stories (happy ones and tricky ones with calm endings). Adults can write their own.

3. Good practice

  • Introduce it at a calm time first, not mid-crisis. Model it yourself: tap a card, try a scene.
  • In a scene, tap Adjust, then Save as my calm, so Calm now opens it in one tap.
  • Keep the same cards in the same places. Hide cards the person doesn't use rather than deleting them.
  • Respect every answer given with the cards, including No, Stop and Don't like.
  • Write down in the care plan which voice, scene and settings work, so every staff member sets it up the same way.

4. Mess-proof by design

Mashing, swiping or many fingers can't break it, change settings or delete anything. Deleting or resetting needs a 2-second press and hold, and the last deleted story can be brought back.

5. Privacy, backups and moving devices

Nothing is ever sent anywhere: photos, stories and settings stay on that one device. Use Save a backup file in Settings before changing or resetting a device, and keep the file somewhere safe and private.

6. If something isn't right

  • No sound? Check Hush is off (every screen with sound has a Hush button; it says Hush on when sound is off) and the device isn't on silent. Sound starts after the first tap (a browser rule).
  • Voice sounds robotic? Cards and stories an adult has written use the device's own voice. Built-in words use the recorded voice chosen in Settings.
  • Looks out of date? Close it fully and open it again while online; it updates itself.

Calm Space is a support tool, not a medical or therapy device. It doesn't replace a speech and language therapist, OT or other professional advice.
